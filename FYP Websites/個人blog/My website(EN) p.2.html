Episode 1: Five Taste

This is the kind of cha chaan teng that makes me want to wake up early — or drop by for breakfast even after a late night.

Breakfast Recommendation

Photo 5: Satay Beef Noodles (original soup base) - Breakfast noodles $40

Satay Beef Noodles

This is a classic Hong Kong cha chaan teng dish which served with instant noodles in a peanut-based satay soup with sliced beef. This is also their signature dish. It's different from other cha chaan tengs. As soon as it's served, the rich satay smell hits you.

The owner told me that he added a Thai twist to the Hong Kong recipe. The peanut sauce is rich and flavorful which makes the soup deeper and thicker than usual. You can really taste the peanut aroma when you are having it. The beef itself is tender and chewy. I order this almost every time. It's really good. You can also try it with a tomato soup base for no extra cost. It's just as good. Sour, sweet, and very appetizing. It goes well with the satay beef.

Photo 6: Crispy Bun (+$3 upgrade from regular toast)

Crispy Bun with Condensed Milk & Butter

This is a signature Hong Kong cha chaan teng snack. The regular set comes with a slice of Danish toast with butter. For an extra $3, you can upgrade to this bun. The outside is toasted until crispy. Covered in condensed milk. It's pretty good.

Lunch recommendations

Photo 7: Deep-Fried Pork Cutlet (Snacks) $28

Deep-Fried Pork Cutlet

It doesn't seem anything special at first glance. But once you cut into it, you can immediately tell the meat and the crust are neither soggy nor mushy at all. Take a bite. It is super satisfying. Dipping it in a bit of salad dressing makes it even better.The dish is not just for lunch. You can order it anytime. I would say it is one of the best deep-fried pork cutlets you will find.

As a popular Hong Kong slang would describe, this is really dope. The owner also shared that he spent a lot of time and effort in perfecting this dish.I really recommend giving their pork cutlet a try.

My Rating

Taste: ⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️

Value for money: ⭐️⭐️⭐️

Photo 8: Signature Five-Taste Grill (choose any two items) $56

Signature Five-Taste Grill

This is a set where you pick two items. I always go for the fried pork cutlet and the deep-fried chicken leg. The pork cutlet is as good as always. As for the chicken leg. It is freshly fried, not pre-cooked. The skin is so crispy that you can hear the sound of your own biting while it is still juicy inside and not dry at all.

I chose rice as my side this time. The plate also came with a small amount of vegetables and French fries. The portion was honestly insane. I had to grab another plate just to have enough space to enjoy the chicken leg. I was in a food coma after finishing.

My Rating

Taste: ⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️

Value for money: ⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️

Afternoon Tea Recommendation

Photo 9: Pork Chop Bun (Afternoon tea set $40 / Snacks $28)

Pork Chop Bun

Besides their deep-fried pork cutlet, pan-fried pork chop here is another specialty. Tender and well-seasoned. It is served in a toasted crispy bread roll with a bit of salad dressing. Simple and delicious.

The portion is generous and offers great value. During afternoon tea hours, you can add extra $8 for a mini noodle allowing you to mix and match your favourites. Such as satay beef macaroni or spiced pork cubes instant noodles. The choice is yours.

My Rating

Taste: ⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️

Value for money: ⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️
